# Relyvora

**A Social Escrow & Trust Layer**  
*(Eliminating "What I Ordered vs. What I Got" in Social Commerce)*

---

## 💡 Overview & Value Proposition
In emerging markets like Nigeria, Ghana, and Kenya, social commerce across platforms such as WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and Snapchat represents a multi-billion dollar economy. However, fraud and lack of buyer-seller trust remain the primary bottlenecks.

**Relyvora** sits directly on the transaction flow, providing:
- **Instant Buyer Trust:** Sellers can place verified escrow checkout links directly in their social media bios, chats, and posts.
- **Fair Protection:** Funds remain safely held until delivery is confirmed and validated against what was ordered.
- **Zero Inventory / Low Overhead:** No inventory or logistics assets needed—the platform focuses purely on escrow, verification, and payment settlement.

---

## 💰 Monetization & Business Model
- **Take Rate:** ~3% fee per completed transaction.
- **Organic Distribution:** Vendors market the tool themselves by displaying verified trust badges and escrow links to increase their own conversion rates.
