# Relyvora Foundation: Inventory of Removed Claims & Editorial Audit

This document records all unverified statistics, fabricated case studies, unauthorized institutional references, and absolute marketing claims removed from `foundation.relyvora.com` during the Foundation Rebuild (Branch `foundation-v2`).

---

## 1. Editorial Rationale

Relyvora Foundation is in the **planning and partner-design phase**. Presenting speculative targets as historical achievements, quoting fictitious merchants, or referencing global multilateral institutions without formal agreements damages institutional credibility, creates consumer protection risks, and violates truth-in-advertising standards.

The page has been reset to an honest, qualitative statement of intent:
> **"Relyvora Foundation is a planned initiative of Relyvora Technologies. We want to combine practical digital selling skills with safer payments for women and young entrepreneurs."**

## 4. Retained Factual Baseline

The rebuild retains only verifiable, honest facts:
- Relyvora Foundation is a **planned initiative** of Relyvora Technologies Limited (RC 9982218).
- The initiative aims to address real challenges faced by informal micro-merchants on WhatsApp and Instagram (risk of fake alerts, failed deliveries, and loss of working capital).
- Planned offerings focus on three areas: **digital selling skills**, **money basics**, and **safer payments**.
- The Foundation is actively seeking dialogue with funders, corporate sponsors, and mentors.
- Supports the directional goals of **UN Sustainable Development Goals 5 (Gender Equality)** and **8 (Decent Work & Economic Growth)**.
