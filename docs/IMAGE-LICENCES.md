## 2. Guidelines for Adding New Photography

Before committing any photographic asset to `/public/`:
1. Ensure the image has been optimized into WebP or AVIF format under 200 KB according to [`docs/IMAGE-GUIDE.md`](./IMAGE-GUIDE.md).
2. Append a row to the table above detailing the licensing terms.
3. Verify that the asset does not portray identifiable people without a signed model release, or note editorial-only constraints.
